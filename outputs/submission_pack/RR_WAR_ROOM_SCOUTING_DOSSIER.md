## 1. Executive Scouting Charter & Tactical Gap Diagnosis

In Season 1, Rajasthan Royals backed our analytical recommendation of **Vignesh Puthur**, securing him at the auction. For Season 2, our mandate expands to **solving RR's high-leverage execution gaps** heading into the mega auction cycle for IPL 2027.

### Existing RR Core Diagnostics & Squad Gaps:
1. **Top-Order Luxury vs. Lower-Order Fragility**: RR's retained core (Yashasvi Jaiswal, Sanju Samson, Riyan Parag, Dhruv Jurel, Shimron Hetmyer) is exceptional, but historical data shows close losses in chases where the required rate exceeded 11.5 RPO in overs 16–20. RR requires a **dedicated domestic death finisher** at #6/#7 so Hetmyer and Jurel are not forced to manufacture boundaries under isolated pressure.
2. **Death Overs Run Leakage**: Defending 180+ at Sawai Mansingh Stadium (Jaipur) requires extreme restrictiveness. While Sandeep Sharma is masterful, RR needs a second death bowler (spin or pace) who commands sub-7.5 economy when opposition teams target the short straight or square boundaries.
3. **Home Venue Dynamics (SMS Stadium, Jaipur)**: Jaipur features 68–74m square boundaries and a pitch that grips in the middle overs. Our scouting model heavily weights restrictive dot-ball spinners and agile running batters.
